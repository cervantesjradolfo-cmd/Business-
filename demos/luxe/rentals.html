Good to know

Rental FAQs

How much space do I need?

Plan for the unit's size plus about 5 feet on every side, and an outdoor outlet within 50 feet. We can bring a generator if you don't have power nearby.

Can you set up on concrete or indoors?

Yes! We use sandbags instead of stakes on concrete, asphalt, or indoor floors. Just check the ceiling height for indoor events.

What if it rains?

Safety first. If there's rain or strong wind in the forecast, you can reschedule for free or get a full refund.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

A small deposit holds your date, and you pay the rest on party day. Deposits are refundable for weather cancellations.

How long is a rental?

Standard rentals are up to 8 hours on the same day. Overnight and multi-day rentals are available. Just ask!
