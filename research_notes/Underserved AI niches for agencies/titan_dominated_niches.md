# Titan-Dominated Niches: Where Vertical Platforms Already Bundle AI Receptionist / Chat / Follow-up (US, as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-17. Scope: 15 web searches and no full-page fetches, so every finding is based on search-result snippets and summaries. Many sources are vendor marketing or competitor/affiliate comparison pages, and each one is labeled. Wherever this file says "vendor claim", treat the item as unverified.

## Home services (HVAC / plumbing / electrical): ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro

### Takeaway
This is the most contested niche. All three major field-service platforms now sell native AI receptionist or CSR products, and those products book jobs straight onto their own dispatch boards. ServiceTitan's AI is premium and still has low adoption (about 700 "Max" locations expected by the end of FY27). Jobber and Housecall Pro sell AI as paid add-ons, not as standard features. An outside agency's main handicap is write access to the scheduling and dispatch system: an outside agency can't book directly onto the calendar the way the native add-on can.

### Cited Findings
- **ServiceTitan AI Voice Agents.** ServiceTitan promoted them in a January 8, 2026 webinar, and an April 17, 2026 recap called them its newest product. They handle overflow calls, after-hours calls and routine scheduling, and they work with ServiceTitan's Contact Center Pro or with a third-party phone system. — [ServiceTitan blog recap](https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/webinar-recap-ai-voice-agents-call-booking); [webinar](https://www.servicetitan.com/webinar/ai-voice-agent)
- **ServiceTitan setup time and rollout.** An August 2026 ServiceTitan podcast says the agents gained new booking skills and faster setup. One customer said setup took about 30 minutes, compared with weeks for a third-party answering service. An August 27, 2026 episode featured a Connecticut trade business that had gone live with its first Virtual Agent. — [ServiceTitan podcast](https://www.servicetitan.com/podcasts/mastering-servicetitan/lindsey-grwitz-interview); [podcast 2](https://www.servicetitan.com/podcasts/mastering-servicetitan/amanda-root-interview) (vendor-owned)
- **ServiceTitan "Virtual Agent" ship date.** An aggregator lists a "ServiceTitan Pro AI Virtual Agent" that books onto the dispatch board and can transfer to a human, with a "shipped" date of July 15, 2026. That date is unconfirmed. — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/de/servicetitan-pro-ai-virtual-agent)
- **ServiceTitan scale (FY27 Q2, reported around Sept 2026).** Revenue was $292.8M, up 21% year over year. Gross transaction volume was $26.8B, up 17%, with "softer customer job and lead growth." FY27 revenue guidance is $1.139B–$1.144B. — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/servicetitan-q2-revenue-rises-21-to-292-8-million-as-agentic-ai-adoption-expands/); [Nasdaq call highlights](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/servicetitan-q2-earnings-call-highlights); [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/transcript-servicetitan-q2-earnings-call-highlights-2026-09-08/)
- **ServiceTitan "Max".** Max is an "agentic operating system" with more than 30 AI capabilities, covering demand generation, booking, field sales and more. Max locations more than doubled in Q2, and ServiceTitan expects more than 700 enrolled Max locations by the end of FY27. It has started selling Max to select new customers and plans smaller Max packages. Free onboarding and the billing ramp create a $4–5M revenue headwind. — [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/servicetitan-q2-earnings-call-highlights); [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/servicetitan-q2-revenue-rises-21-to-292-8-million-as-agentic-ai-adoption-expands/)
- **Jobber AI Receptionist.** It is a paid add-on, and its price is reported inconsistently: $29/mo in one competitor's citation of Jobber's pricing page, and $99/mo on the Grow plan in another source. Third-party reviews say it is included on the Plus plan ($599/mo) and unavailable on lower tiers. A Jobber community post mentions a 3-month promotional price that ran until March 31. — [Beside blog (competitor)](https://www.beside.com/blog/jobber-ai-phone-answering); [Jobber community forum](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/spring-feature-announcement/customer-q-ai-receptionist-is-the-pricing-im-seeing-on-my-grow-plan-permanent-/2436); [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/en/jobber-ai-receptionist)
- **Housecall Pro CSR AI.** CSR AI is an add-on on every tier, and its price is quote-only. One third-party estimate puts it at $100–150/mo, which is unverified. Base plans run about $59 (Basic), $149 (Essentials) and $299 (MAX) per month on annual billing. — [ContractorToolStack](https://contractortoolstack.com/software/housecall-pro/pricing/); [BuyerSprint](https://buyersprint.com/2026/04/18/housecall-pro-pricing-2026/)
- **Housecall Pro roadmap.** The February 2026 product update listed AI Team multi-chat, CSR AI improvements (booking fallback and call handling) and "Voice to invoice". The Spring Summit on June 4, 2026 was billed as its "largest AI product release to date". The fall 2025 release included the "AI Team", CSR AI and Analyst AI. — [Housecall Pro Feb 2026 updates](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/february-2026-product-updates/); [Landscape Management](https://www.landscapemanagement.net/housecall-pro-to-unveil-platform-redesign-at-built-to-last-spring-summit-2026/); [PMmag fall 2025](https://www.pmmag.com/articles/106942-housecall-pro-unveils-major-ai-powered-updates-for-fall-2025)

### Inferences
- ServiceTitan customers, mostly larger shops with $1M+ in revenue, are a poor target. The vendor's AI books natively, sets up in about 30 minutes (per its own claim), and is pushed by the account team. Expecting only about 700 Max locations, against a customer base in the thousands, means most ServiceTitan customers have not yet adopted its premium AI. That leaves a window, but it is closing.
- For Jobber and Housecall Pro users, the native add-ons (about $29–150/mo) set a low price anchor, below the $297–497/mo retainers typical of GHL agencies. An agency needs to differentiate on outcomes such as reactivation campaigns, reviews and ads plus follow-up, rather than on the receptionist alone.
- Small trades shops that use no FSM software, or that run on spreadsheets or QuickBooks, are not locked in. That segment is likely easier to win.

### Gaps
- Avoca and other venture-backed HVAC voice startups were not researched (out of budget).
- ServiceTitan's AI Voice Agent pricing was not found.
- Whether the Housecall Pro June 2026 release shipped, and what it included, is unconfirmed.
- Customer counts for Jobber and Housecall Pro were not verified in this pass.

## Dental and medical: Weave, NexHealth, Podium, Adit, EHR-embedded AI

### Takeaway
Weave is the dental-specific titan. The ADA's Member Advantage program endorsed it in March 2026. Its AI receptionist is a separate paid add-on, was reportedly still in beta, and supports only Open Dental, Dentrix and Eaglesoft. NexHealth's AI is mostly web scheduling plus an API that third parties can build voice agents on. In practice this makes NexHealth an integration enabler for an agency more than a competitor. Practice-management (PMS) write-back is the main moat.

### Cited Findings
- **ADA endorsement.** ADA Member Advantage endorsed Weave as its patient engagement platform in March 2026. — [ADA News](https://adanews.ada.org/ada-news/2026/march/ada-member-advantage-endorses-weave-as-patient-engagement-platform/)
- **Weave AI receptionist status.** It is a separate paid add-on covering voice and text. It was reportedly in beta and supported only Open Dental, Dentrix and Eaglesoft. — [Quo blog (competitor)](https://www.quo.com/blog/weave-pricing/); [Ainora (competitor)](https://ainora.lt/blog/weave-ai-receptionist-alternatives-2026)
- **Weave pricing.** The base platform starts at about $199/mo (Pro tier), and higher tiers are quote-only. A Massachusetts Dental Society partner page lists an activation fee of $1,950, discounted to $500 for members. — [PupPilot](https://www.puppilot.co/phone-systems/weave); [MassDental](https://www.massdental.org/partners/weave/)
- **Market price range.** A third-party estimate puts dental-specific AI receptionists that book into the PMS at about $300–900 per location per month. — [S. Bhattacharya blog](https://sagnikbhattacharya.com/blog/ai-receptionist-cost-dental-practice-2026)
- **Weave ranked first.** A 2026 comparison ranks Weave AI Phone as its top pick. — [Layer3Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/comparisons/best-ai-receptionist-for-dental-offices)
- **NexHealth.**
  - A third-party comparison characterizes its AI as web-based scheduling rather than phone answering, at about $350–700/mo. Those figures are mid-2026 estimates. — [Layer3Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/comparisons/best-ai-receptionist-for-dental-offices)
  - NexHealth's own case study shows a dentist who built English and Spanish AI receptionists in 14 days on the NexHealth Synchronizer API plus VAPI. — [NexHealth case study](https://synchronizer.nexhealth.com/case-studies/dr-zarger)

### Inferences
- Dental is crowded. Weave has institutional distribution through the ADA and state societies, and its pricing ($199+/mo plus activation) already bundles phones and texting.
- An agency can still compete by using NexHealth's Synchronizer API to get PMS write-back, which removes the integration moat. The cost is extra per-practice API fees and HIPAA obligations: the agency needs a BAA, and GHL's HIPAA posture needs checking.
- Medical, meaning EHR-embedded AI from Epic or athena, mainly affects larger groups. Small independent clinics were not verified in this pass.

### Gaps
- No data was found on Adit's AI phone product.
- Podium's dental and medical AI was not specifically researched.
- No Epic, athenahealth or eClinicalWorks AI-agent details were found within the budget.
- Weave's AI add-on price is not public.

## Restaurants: Toast, SoundHound, Loman, Slang.ai, OpenTable/Resy

### Takeaway
Toast, the dominant SMB restaurant POS, does not build its own phone AI. Instead it runs a partner ecosystem: SoundHound, Incept AI and Loman have direct order injection into Toast. Toast's support documentation says the Incept capability is included free with the base package. That makes AI phone ordering close to a commodity for Toast restaurants, and a poor fit for a GHL-style agency.

### Cited Findings
- **SoundHound.** Its partnership with Toast lets phone orders flow into the Toast POS. It updates menus in real time and texts a payment link to the caller. SoundHound also runs phone ordering for Torchy's Tacos (130 locations). — [Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/working-with-toast-soundhound-enables-voice-ordering-over-the-phone-for-restaurants/); [Digital Transactions 2](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/toast-and-uber-expand-a-delivery-partnership-soundhound-rolls-out-ai-ordering-at-torchys-tacos/)
- **Incept AI.** It is a Toast integration that answers calls and pushes pickup orders to the kitchen display and POS. Phone payment is "coming". Toast's support documentation says the capability is included free with the base package. — [Toast Support](https://support.toasttab.com/article/Get-Started-With-InceptAI-Integration)
- **Drive-thru.** Toast's drive-thru launch named Incept as an AI voice ordering partner, and more AI voice partners are expected in 2026. — [Nasdaq press release](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/toast-launches-unified-drive-thru-solution-modernize-enterprise-quick-service)
- **Loman AI.** Loman joined the Toast Partner Ecosystem in July 2026. It handles full orders with modifiers and payments. Its claim of a $199/mo flat fee is vendor marketing. — [Verdict Foodservice](https://www.verdictfoodservice.com/newsletters/loman-ai-integrates-toast-streamline-restaurant-phone-orders); [Loman blog](https://loman.ai/blog/toast-restaurant-phone-answering-ai)

### Inferences
- If Toast includes a voice AI with its base package, a $297+/mo agency receptionist is hard to sell to a Toast restaurant. Restaurants also have thin margins and high churn.
- The agency opportunity is limited to reputation and review management and catering or event lead follow-up, not phone ordering.

### Gaps
- No data was found on OpenTable/Resy AI phone products or on Slang.ai's 2026 position.
- The scope of "free with base package" for Incept should be confirmed by fetching the Toast support page.

## Real estate: Follow Up Boss, BoldTrail/kvCORE, Lofty

### Takeaway
Real estate CRMs are entrenched, and agents and teams lean heavily on their native follow-up automation. Lofty markets an "AI Assistant" and an "AI Workforce" for lead nurture. Follow Up Boss's strength is integration with more than 250 lead sources. This pass did not verify 2026 AI voice features for Follow Up Boss or BoldTrail.

### Cited Findings
- **Lofty.** Its AI assistant nurtures inactive leads with conversational follow-up, alongside IDX search. Its "AI Workforce" covers automation and virtual-assistant features. Pricing is cited as starting at $449/mo for up to 3 users, though sources conflict. — [DMChamp roundup](https://dmchamp.com/best/best-ai-tools-for-real-estate-2026/); [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-crm-for-real-estate)
- **Follow Up Boss.** Called "best CRM for most agents/teams" in 2026, with 250+ lead-source integrations and pricing of about $69/user/mo (Grow). On Capterra it is rated 4.5 (57 reviews) against Lofty's 3.9 (127 reviews). — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-crm-for-real-estate); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.co.uk/compare/130020/157457/follow-up-boss/vs/lofty)
- **BoldTrail (kvCORE).** Aimed at brokerages and large teams at about $499+/mo. No 2026 AI detail was found. — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-crm-for-real-estate)

### Inferences
- Individual agents are price-sensitive, and most already pay for a CRM with drip and AI follow-up. GHL-based agencies do sell into real estate, but it is saturated with GHL-reseller competition as well as the titans.

### Gaps
- No 2026 AI voice or ISA features were verified for Follow Up Boss (owned by Zillow) or BoldTrail (owned by Inside Real Estate).
- Market share figures were not found.

## Auto dealers: Podium, Impel, CDK

### Takeaway
This niche is dominated by the titans; avoid it. Podium says 25% of US dealerships use its AI Employee "Jerry". Impel offers an end-to-end automotive AI platform. Service-department voice AI books directly into the dealer's service scheduler (Xtime) and needs deep DMS/CRM integration.

### Cited Findings
- **Podium Jerry.** Jerry answers phones, chat, text and email, recommends inventory, and books test drives and service appointments into Xtime. Podium's automotive GM said at NADA 2026 that 25% of US dealerships use Jerry. Podium also claims more than $30B in influenced sales and 4.5M+ leads handled. These are vendor claims. — [NADA](https://www.nada.org/nada-show/nada-headlines/podiums-ross-tinkham-presents-ai-strategies-dealers-2026); [Podium Auto](https://www.podium.com/experience/auto)
- **Impel.** Impel describes itself as an "AI Operating System" for automotive retail, with conversational phone and messaging AI and service scheduling. It acquired Outsell for more than $100M in June 2024. — [Stork.ai compare](https://www.stork.ai/compare/podium-vs-impel)

### Inferences
- The DMS/CRM integration barrier (CDK, Reynolds, Xtime) plus Podium's 25% penetration makes this niche one to avoid for a solo agency.

### Gaps
- CDK's AI products were not found.

## Legal: Clio, Smith.ai, Lawmatics

### Takeaway
This is moderately contested. In these results, Clio's intake product (Clio Grow) did not include its own AI receptionist. Clio mostly relies on integrated third parties: Smith.ai (a hybrid of AI and human agents, from $300/mo) and the intake CRM Lawmatics. That leaves room for an agency that syncs to Clio, but the price anchor of about $100–400/mo is already set.

### Cited Findings
- **Smith.ai.** It offers an AI receptionist with live-agent backup, and its virtual receptionist plans start at $300/mo. It integrates with Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther and Lawmatics, and the State Bar of Michigan advertises a member discount. — [Clutch/Smith.ai](https://clutch.co/go-to-review/smithai/147707); [State Bar of Michigan](https://www.michbar.org/News/NewsDetail/nid/6281)
- **Lawmatics.** Lawmatics positions itself as a legal CRM with native intake, qualification, scheduling and follow-up. This comes from its own guide. — [Lawmatics](https://www.lawmatics.com/blog/best-ai-receptionists-for-law-firms)
- **Clio-integrated receptionists.** Legal AI receptionists that sync to Clio mostly start at about $100–400/mo. Clio's product line includes Manage and Grow. — [Stork.ai blog](https://www.stork.ai/blog/ai-receptionist-for-law-firms-2026); [NextPhone](https://www.getnextphone.com/blog/clio-ai-receptionist-integration-matter-creation)

### Inferences
- The legal niche has no single native-AI titan, but it does have strong specialist incumbents and bar-endorsed vendors. Firms with high case values, such as personal injury or immigration, pay well for intake speed. The integration needed is Clio's API, which is open.

### Gaps
- Whether Clio launched a native AI receptionist in 2026 was not confirmed.

## Salons, spas and fitness: Mindbody/Booker, Zenoti, Vagaro, Boulevard, Fresha

### Takeaway
The main booking platforms are shipping native AI front desks. Zenoti has had an AI Receptionist for voice calls since Sept 30, 2025. Mindbody/Booker has Messenger[ai], an AI front desk for missed-call text-back, SMS and webchat. Vagaro has AI chat ("Vera"). Booking-system write-back is the moat, and these features overlap directly with the agency offer of missed-call text-back plus AI chat.

### Cited Findings
- **Zenoti.** Zenoti launched its "AI Workforce" with an AI Receptionist and AI Concierge on September 30, 2025, with more agents to follow. The receptionist handles booking, rescheduling and questions, and connects to the appointment book, memberships and POS. — [Zenoti press](https://www.zenoti.com/press/zenoti-launches-industrys-first-ai-workforce-for-the-beauty-wellness-and-fitness-industries); [Aesthetics Journal](https://aestheticsjournal.com/news/zenoti-unveils-new-ai-receptionist)
- **Mindbody/Booker Messenger[ai].** It provides missed-call text-back, booking, and SMS and webchat answers as a "24/7 AI front desk". Dated 2021 pricing was $99/mo per location for basic two-way messaging and $199/mo for premium. — [Mindbody Booker](https://www.mindbodyonline.com/booker); [AthleTech News (2021)](https://athletechnews.com/messengerai-receptionist/)
- **Vagaro.** Vagaro has an AI chat product, "Vera Receptionist". Per Zenoti's comparison page, updated Oct 7, 2026, voice AI for Booker and Vagaro is "not stated publicly". This is a competitor's claim. — [Zenoti compare](https://www.zenoti.com/compare/booker-vs-zenoti-vs-vagaro)
- **Third-party add-ons.** BookingBee.ai is a third-party voice AI that integrates with Mindbody and Booker. — [Salon Today](https://www.salontoday.com/products/bookingbeeai)

### Inferences
- Missed-call text-back and AI chat are already bundled for Mindbody, Booker and Zenoti users, so the core GHL offer adds little there. Voice AI may still be a gap on Vagaro and Booker. Independent salons that book through cheap or free tools such as Fresha or Square are price-sensitive.

### Gaps
- Nothing was found on Boulevard's or Fresha's AI receptionist.
- ABC Glofox (fitness) was not researched.
- Current Messenger[ai] pricing was not found.
- Med spas (Aesthetic Record, Moxie) were not researched.

## Veterinary: Weave, PetDesk

### Takeaway
There is no clear native-AI titan here. Sources conflict on whether PetDesk offers an AI receptionist, and the more specific review says it does not. Point-solution AI startups (CallJess, VetDodo, VetPawer, DeskBuddy) are flooding in at low prices, some as low as about $40/mo.

### Cited Findings
- **PetDesk.** One review documents no AI call answering; PetDesk's AI is limited to call summaries and a documentation tool. Another blog lists "PetDesk AI" at custom pricing. The two conflict. — [PupPilot](https://www.puppilot.co/phone-systems/petdesk); contradicted by [TRTC blog](https://trtc.io/blog/details/ai-receptionist-vets-2026)
- **Vet AI startups.** CallJess, VetDodo (which books into the PIMS) and VetPawer AVA are all on the market. DeskBuddy is listed at $39.90/mo. — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/collections/ai-receptionists-veterinary); [PupPilot compare](https://puppilot.co/compare/best-ai-receptionist)

### Inferences
- The vet niche has no titan, but cheap specialist startups are compressing prices.

### Gaps
- Weave's vet-specific AI was not verified separately.

## Property management: AppFolio Realm-X, EliseAI, Yardi, Entrata

### Takeaway
This niche is dominated by titans plus a heavily funded AI specialist. AppFolio (20,000+ customers, about 8M units) bundles Realm-X AI agents. EliseAI has more than $100M in ARR and $392M in funding. Yardi and Entrata are building native AI. Avoid it.

### Cited Findings
- **AppFolio.** It has 20,000–22,000+ customers, depending on the source. AI actions in Realm-X grew 7x year over year in Q1 2026, and "Performer" adoption grew nearly 500% quarter over quarter. — [ChartMill](https://www.chartmill.com/news/external/36677/appfolio-realm-x-shows-how-ai-agents-are-reshaping-rentals)
- **EliseAI.** It has more than $100M in ARR and has raised $392M. Yardi and Entrata are building native AI that competes with it. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/eliseai)

### Gaps
- No market share figures were found.
