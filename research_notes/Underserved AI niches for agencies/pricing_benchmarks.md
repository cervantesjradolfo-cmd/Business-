# Pricing Benchmarks: AI Receptionist, AI Chat and Lead Follow-up for US Small Businesses (as of October 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-17. "Viewed" means the page was fetched on that date. Confidence labels: **LIST** = vendor's own pricing page or help doc; **3P** = third-party comparison or review blog (often written by a competing vendor); **ANECDOTE** = agency or vendor marketing claims, guru-style numbers. Most third-party figures conflict a little, so the conflicts are noted.

## 1. Direct-to-SMB AI receptionist prices (DIY/SaaS + human services)

### Takeaway
Self-serve AI receptionists cluster at **$25–$150/mo** entry tiers (about 30–250 calls or minutes), with effective overage of **$0.25–$0.50/min or $0.75–$3.00/call**. Vertical software add-ons are cheaper but locked in: Jobber is $29/mo plus $0.79 per conversation. Human answering services run **$2.30–$5.00/min or $7–$11.50/call**, roughly 10–20x the AI cost per minute. At about 200 calls a month (about 600 minutes), real bills for the SaaS tools land at roughly **$130–$240/mo**.

### Cited Findings
- Modeled bill at 200 calls/mo (~600 min): Goodcall ~$129, Rosie ~$149, Upfirst ~$159.95, My AI Front Desk up to ~$199, Dialzara ~$239. Another comparison at 100 calls/mo has Rosie ~$49 and Upfirst ~$70. The ranking depends on call length — [atsiliepsiu.lt](https://atsiliepsiu.lt/blog/ai-receptionist-pricing-comparison-2026) (3P)
- At 100 calls/mo, Smith.ai is estimated at ~$295 (AI) vs ~$590 (human) — [Loman](https://loman.ai/blog/smith-ai-pricing) (competitor, 3P)
- Market bands: basic $25–$49/mo for 30–60 calls or 60–250 min; premium $150–$300/mo; per-call $0.75–$2.40; per-minute $0.25–$0.48; SaaS setup fees $0 to $50–$200; done-for-you setup $100–$1,500; custom $250–$5,000. Hidden costs: integration fees of $50–$200/mo per integration, and $200–$500 cancellation fees — [getaira](https://www.getaira.io/blog/ai-receptionist-pricing-guide), [bknd](https://bknddevelopment.com/ai/ai-receptionist-cost/) (vendor blogs, 3P)

### Inferences
- An agency's AI receptionist at $297–$497/mo is priced **2–4x the DIY SaaS tools** for the same minutes. It has to win on done-for-you setup, integrations (CRM, calendar, FSM), local niche scripting and reporting, not on raw answering.
- The real price ceiling the agency competes against is the **human answering service** ($250–$720/mo for 50–200 min at Ruby; $300+/mo for 30 calls at Smith.ai human). That is the comparison to use when pitching.
- Jobber's $29 add-on is a pricing floor threat in home services for clients already on Jobber.

### Gaps
- Numa, Housecall Pro CSR AI and Podium AI Employee have no public prices. They are quote-based, and no reliable third-party figure was found.
- Most SaaS prices were not checked against the vendor's own page in this pass. Daber's table (checked 2026-10-02) is a competitor's compilation.

## 2. Build-platform costs per minute (what an agency pays)

### Takeaway
A realistic all-in cost for an inbound receptionist built on Retell, Vapi, ElevenLabs or GHL is **~$0.10–$0.25/min**. A practical planning number is **$0.12–$0.15/min**, plus about $1–$2/mo per phone number. SMS is under 2¢ per segment including carrier fees, before A2P 10DLC registration costs. At 500 min per client per month, hard cost is about **$60–$125/client/mo**.

### Cited Findings
- Example agency cost model: 500 inbound min per client; voice $0.12/min ($60); LC Phone ~$0.015/min ($7.50); number $1.15; GHL Agency Pro $497 and Leadlock Pro $497, each amortized to ~$50/client at 10 clients. Total hard cost ≈ $138/client/mo — [Leadlock, Apr 2026](https://www.leadlock.ai/blog/white-label-ai-voice-agents-gohighlevel-charge-clients/) (vendor, ANECDOTE-grade)
- Owned voice stack ≈ $0.10–$0.15/min all-in, vs platform-metered resale at $0.25 to over $1.00/min (unsourced estimate) — [Allodra](https://www.allodra.ai/blog/gohighlevel-ai-employee-cost) (3P)
- LLM cost per chat conversation is negligible, e.g. ~3,700 tokens ≈ $0.0037 on Claude Haiku 4.5 — [Allodra](https://www.allodra.ai/blog/gohighlevel-ai-employee-cost) citing Anthropic

### Inferences
- The LLM and TTS choice is the biggest cost swing: premium ElevenLabs voices or frontier models can double or triple per-minute cost. Default to $0.015 TTS and a mid-tier model for receptionist work.
- A typical SMB at 200–600 min/mo costs the agency **$25–$90/mo in usage** on Retell/Vapi. Platform subscriptions (GHL $297–$497) are the larger fixed cost until the agency has about 5–10 clients.

### Gaps
- Vapi's and Bland's official pricing pages were not fetched directly; figures are from August 2026 third-party sources.
- GHL's official per-minute Voice AI rate card was not found. The help doc does not list usage rates.
- Twilio's A2P 10DLC brand and campaign registration fee amounts were not captured.

## 3. What agencies charge (setup + retainer + usage, white-label/GHL resale)

### Takeaway
The in-the-wild AI receptionist offer from GHL/white-label agencies is **$297–$997/mo**, with **$497–$600/mo** the most cited middle. Setup fees run **$297–$1,500** for productized offers, or **$1,500–$5,000** for custom automation builds. Agencies price at about **3–5x platform cost**, which works out to roughly 65–80% gross margin. All of this comes from vendors selling to agencies. No independent survey was found.

### Cited Findings
- Example margin: one client at $497/mo nets ~$359 (~72%); 10 clients give $4,970 revenue, ~$1,694 platform cost, ~$3,276 net (~66%) — [Leadlock](https://www.leadlock.ai/blog/white-label-ai-voice-agents-gohighlevel-charge-clients/) (vendor marketing)
- GHL per-location AI fees grow with the client list; one comparison puts the stack at ≈ $970/mo at 10 clients ($497 + per-location AI). AI Employee usage can be rebilled to clients at a markup — [Seldonframe](https://www.seldonframe.com/guides/gohighlevel-saas-mode-vs-flat-pricing) (competitor, 3P); [HighLevel help](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001156-highlevel-pricing-guide) (rebilling, LIST)
- Target niches named by agency vendors: home services, med spas, dental, law, real estate, with no niche-specific prices — [Leadlock](https://www.leadlock.ai/blog/white-label-ai-voice-agents-gohighlevel-charge-clients/)

### Inferences
- A defensible solo-agency ladder, inferred from the above:
  - **$97–$197/mo:** missed-call text-back + chat widget only.
  - **$297–$497/mo:** AI receptionist with booking, CRM, about 500–750 min, and a monthly report.
  - **$597–$997/mo:** adds outbound lead follow-up, review requests, and multi-location.
  - **Setup fee:** $297–$1,500 depending on integrations.
- Price above SaaS ($25–$150), below human answering ($250–$720+), and anchor to the cost of one missed job.
- High-ticket niches (law, HVAC replacement, dental implants, med spa) justify the top of the range. Low-ticket niches (salons, cleaning) face Jobber-style $29 add-ons and $25–$49 SaaS tools.

### Gaps
- **No independent survey** of agency AI receptionist pricing was found. Every agency figure comes from vendors that sell to agencies, or from blogs.
- No sourced per-niche price differences (e.g. law vs plumbing) were found.
- Missed-call text-back sold as a standalone package price was not found in any credible source.

## 4. Human answering-service comparison and the cost of a missed call

### Takeaway
Human answering costs **~$2.30–$5.00/min** or **$7–$11.50/call**. The missed-call value argument is strong but is supported almost entirely by vendor-produced numbers. Present it as a per-client calculation (average job value × close rate), not as an industry statistic.

### Cited Findings
- Human pricing: Ruby $250/50 min to $1,725/500 min (LIST, [Ruby](https://www.ruby.com/pricing/)); Smith.ai human $300/30 calls, $11.50/call overage ([Daber](https://daber.ai/ai-receptionist-pricing)); Posh $2.30/min; Abby Connect $329/100 min ([Daber](https://daber.ai/ai-receptionist-pricing))
- "$126,000/yr lost per small business" is widely repeated. One source attributes it to an AMBS Call Center Aug 2025 analysis (~$12.15 direct cost per missed call). Another builds it from assumptions (88 missed calls/mo × $1,200), so it is **not independently confirmed** — [callsetter.ai](https://callsetter.ai/blog/cost-of-missed-calls), [getaira](https://www.getaira.io/blog/missed-business-calls-statistics) (vendor blogs)
- Miss rates: 25%–40% of inbound calls missed by SMB service businesses; another source says 20%–50% — [Ring-Ready](https://www.ring-ready.com/blog/cost-of-missed-calls-2026), [TeamShift](https://teamshift.io/guides/missed-calls-cost-by-industry-2026) (vendor, low confidence)
- Value per missed call by niche (vendor estimates, wide spread):
  - HVAC: $300–$500 or $1,200–$3,500
  - Plumbing: $350–$500 or $800–$2,000
  - Dental: $653 first-year value, or $4,000–$8,000 lifetime value per new patient
  - Law: $3,000–$50,000+; one calculation gives ~$2,000 expected value at a $5K average case and 40% conversion

  Sources: [VoIP-Int](https://voip-int.com/blog/voip-international-blog-posts-4/hvac-contractors-lose-45k-120k-a-year-to-missed-calls-here-s-the-real-data-273), [TeamShift](https://teamshift.io/guides/missed-calls-cost-by-industry-2026), [Dialfyne](https://dialfyne.com/blog/missed-call-statistics-2026)
- Calculator: at a $4,500 job and 25% close rate, one missed call costs ~$1,125; the median across trades is ~$292, with a range of $99–$1,125 — [Landon Scales](https://www.landonscales.com/tools/missed-call-calculator) (3P)

### Inferences
- At $497/mo, an agency needs to recover **about 1–2 extra jobs a month** in HVAC/plumbing, or a fraction of one case in law, to pay for itself. This is the core ROI pitch.

### Gaps
- No primary, independent study (e.g. academic or BIA/Kelsey-type) on missed-call rates by niche was found in this pass.

## 5. Churn/retention and what SMBs keep paying for

### Takeaway
No hard churn data exists in public sources. Vendor guidance consistently says clients cancel when ROI is invisible, so **automated monthly reporting** (calls answered, leads captured, bookings) is the main retention lever. Full AI answering is likely stickier than text-back alone.

### Cited Findings
- The three churn drivers named are ROI invisibility, onboarding friction, and pricing misalignment. The suggested fix is automated monthly reports on missed and handled calls and bookings — [VoiceAI Connect](https://www.myvoiceaiconnect.com/blog/how-to-scale-ai-receptionist-agency-past-20-clients) (vendor, ANECDOTE)
- "Not seeing results" is the top reason clients fire agencies. A white-label portal and stale-login alerts (no login for over 2 weeks) are used to flag at-risk accounts — [NetPartners](https://netpartners.marketing/agency-client-churn-white-label-client-portal-highlevel-saas-mode/) (ANECDOTE)
- Missed-call text-back is the cheap entry point and is claimed to recover 15–30% of missed calls into bookings (unsourced). Critics note the customer still has to reply and explain the job — [Upfirst](https://upfirst.ai/solutions/missed-call-text-back), [Joist](https://www.joist.com/workshop/technology/ai-receptionist-missed-calls-into-leads/) (vendor)
- Hidden-cost complaints in SaaS (per-call add-ons, $200–$500 termination fees, integration fees) are common friction points — [bknd](https://bknddevelopment.com/ai/ai-receptionist-cost/), [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/smith-ai-pricing-explained)

### Inferences
- Bundle reporting into every tier. Lead with text-back as a low-cost wedge, then upsell full AI answering. Avoid long contracts and termination fees, since SaaS competitors are criticized for them; month-to-month with a setup fee is a typical agency pattern.

### Gaps
- No credible churn or retention statistics (monthly churn %, average client lifetime) for AI receptionist agencies or SaaS were found.
- No data was found on which specific features (text-back vs voice vs chat vs reviews) SMBs retain longest.
