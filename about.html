Our story

It all started with one bouncy castle

Benji's Jumpers began with a single bounce house and a big idea: party rentals should be easy, clean, and fun for the whole family.

Today we bring smiles to birthdays, block parties, school field days, church picnics, and community events all over the area. We still treat every booking like it's for our own family.
