    Questions

    Good to know

    How much space do I need?

    Plan for the rental's size plus about 5 feet on every side, on fairly flat ground. You'll need an outlet within 50 feet, or add a generator.

    Can you set up on concrete?

    Yes. We use heavy sandbags instead of stakes on concrete, asphalt, or turf.

    What if it rains?

    Safety comes first. If rain or high winds are forecast, you can reschedule for free or get your deposit back.

    When do you arrive?

    We usually arrive 1–2 hours before your party starts so everything's ready before guests show up.

    Do I need to pay up front?

    A small deposit holds your date. You pay the rest on party day.
